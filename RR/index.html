Help choose an advert

A major homebuilder is launching a campaign this autumn. The adverts are aimed at people who might be interested in buying a new-build home, and are designed to get them to register their interest now so they could be moved in before Christmas. They have several designs and need to know which one works best. That's where you come in.

One thing worth knowing as you look at them: buying a new-build home is usually quicker than buying an ordinary second-hand home, because there's no chain of buyers and sellers to wait for. A new-build move can often complete in around two months, where a purchase with a chain typically takes many months.

With that in mind:

  • You'll see 2 pairs of adverts
  • Each advert has several panels — please look at the images, text, and how they work together
  • In reality these would appear as a short animation (GIF), but here they're shown as a series of images so you can view each step clearly
  • For each pair, you'll rate both adverts and pick which one you think is more effective
  • There are no right or wrong answers

You can tap any image to enlarge it.
